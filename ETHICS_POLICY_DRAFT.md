## Page Copy

### Academic Integrity & Publication Ethics

Scholar Perspective is committed to upholding the highest standards of academic integrity and publication ethics. This page outlines the principles all authors, reviewers, and editors are expected to follow.

**Originality**

All submissions must represent the author's own original work. Manuscripts that have been previously published, or that are under simultaneous consideration elsewhere, will not be accepted. Authors are responsible for ensuring that all sources, data, and ideas drawn from other works are properly cited and attributed.

**Plagiarism**

Plagiarism in any form — including the unattributed use of another author's words, ideas, or data — is not tolerated. All submissions are checked for originality prior to acceptance, using plagiarism-detection software together with review by a member of our Advisory Board. Manuscripts found to contain plagiarized content will be rejected, and any concerns identified after publication will be investigated and may result in a correction or retraction.

**Authorship**

Authorship should be limited to individuals who have made a meaningful contribution to the conception, research, or writing of the submitted work. All listed authors must agree to the final manuscript prior to submission.

**Conflicts of Interest**

Authors, reviewers, and editorial board members are expected to disclose any financial, personal, or professional relationships that could be perceived to influence the review or publication of a submission. Board members do not review manuscripts they have authored or co-authored.

**Data and Source Integrity**

Authors must accurately represent their sources and data. Fabrication, falsification, or misrepresentation of research findings is grounds for rejection or retraction.

**Addressing Concerns**

Any concerns regarding a potential violation of these principles — whether raised before or after publication — will be reviewed by the editorial team. Depending on the outcome, this may result in a request for revision, a published correction, or retraction of the article.

Scholar Perspective follows the core practices set out by the [Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE)](https://publicationethics.org/).
