### Editorial Policies

Scholar Perspective is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal publishing research in the arts and humanities. This page describes how submissions are reviewed, how editorial decisions are made, and the terms under which articles are published.

**Peer Review Process**

Every article published in Scholar Perspective is reviewed prior to publication by at least one member of our Advisory Board with expertise relevant to the submission. Additional reviewers may be consulted when a submission spans multiple fields or when further evaluation is needed.

Scholar Perspective uses a single-blind review model: reviewers are aware of the author's identity, while the identity of the reviewer is not disclosed to the author.

Reviewers assess each submission for scholarly merit, originality, clarity of argument, appropriate use of sources, and fit with the journal's scope. Based on this assessment, reviewers recommend one of the following outcomes:

- Accept
- Accept with revisions
- Revise and resubmit
- Reject

Authors whose submissions require revision will receive the reviewers' comments and are invited to submit a revised manuscript.

**Editorial Decisions**

Final decisions on publication are made by the Editor-in-Chief, [NAME], on the basis of the reviewers' recommendations.

**Conflicts of Interest in Review**

Reviewers do not evaluate submissions with which they have a personal, professional, or financial conflict of interest. When a member of the Advisory Board submits their own work, it is reviewed by another board member with no involvement in the submission, and the authoring member takes no part in the editorial decision on that work.

**Open Access and Licensing**

All articles in Scholar Perspective are freely available to read online immediately upon publication, with no subscription or embargo.

Authors retain copyright of their work. Unless otherwise noted on an individual article, articles are published under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/), which permits others to share and adapt the work for non-commercial purposes, provided appropriate credit is given to the author(s) and the original publication in Scholar Perspective.

**Publication Ethics**

Scholar Perspective follows the core practices of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). For our policies on originality, plagiarism, authorship, and research integrity, please see our [Academic Integrity & Publication Ethics] page.
